FAQ

Questions, answered.

Will it sound like a robot?

No. Each system is set up with a voice guide based on how you actually write, and replies use the details your customer gave. You review samples before anything goes live.

Do I need new software?

Usually not. We build on tools you already have, like your email, Google account and website forms.

Do I stay in control?

Always. You approve how it writes, can require approval before messages send, and can pause it anytime.

What about my customers' data?

Your leads and logs stay in your own accounts. Lead details are sent to the AI only to write your replies, and are never sold or shared.

Can I cancel?

Yes. There are no long contracts. Ongoing support is month to month.

Is this only for real estate?

No. Real estate is where we started, but the same systems work for any business that gets inquiries, bookings or reviews.
